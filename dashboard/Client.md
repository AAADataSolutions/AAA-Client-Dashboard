Client Dashboard Features
Dashboard
Client Overview Dashboard — Gives the client a quick overview of their entire organization.
Organization Statistics — Shows properties, services, open tickets, onboarding and other key numbers.
Ticket Analytics — Graphs showing open, closed, pending and resolved tickets over different time periods.
Time-Based Analytics — View activity/ticket statistics for today, this week, this month or custom periods.
Recent Activity — Shows the latest updates and actions related to the organization.
Attention Required — Highlights pending E911, onboarding, porting or support issues requiring attention.
Properties
Property Management — View all hotels/properties belonging to the organization.
Property Details — View complete information about an individual property.
Property-Wise Overview — See services, lines, E911, onboarding and tickets for a specific property.
Property Search & Filters — Quickly find a specific property among dozens or hundreds of properties.
Services & Lines
Service Inventory — View all services/lines associated with the organization.
Service-Type Filtering — Filter services by voice, emergency, elevator, fax, etc.
Property-Wise Service View — See exactly which services belong to each property.
Service Status Tracking — View whether a service is active, inactive, pending, etc.
E911 / Emergency
E911 Status Dashboard — Quickly see verified, pending and problematic properties.
Property-Level E911 Information — View emergency information associated with each property.
Verification Tracking — Track the verification status of emergency services.
Onboarding
Onboarding Tracker — Track the progress of properties being onboarded.
Onboarding Status Timeline — See stages such as pending, in progress, testing and completed.
Target Date Tracking — See expected completion dates for onboarding.
Porting
Porting Tracker — Track numbers/services currently going through the porting process.
Porting Status — See the current stage of each porting request.
Porting Timeline — View target and completion dates.
Support
Support Ticket System — Client can raise and manage support requests directly from the portal.
Ticket Creation — Create a ticket with property, issue, priority and description.
Ticket Conversation — Communicate with the admin/support team within the ticket.
Ticket Attachments — Attach screenshots, documents or other relevant files.
Ticket Status Tracking — See whether a ticket is open, in progress, waiting, resolved or closed.
Ticket History — View previously submitted and resolved tickets.
Ticket Analytics — Visualize ticket volume and resolution trends.
Email Ticket
Account
Profile Management — Client users can update their account information.
Organization Users — Authorized organization users can be managed according to their permissions.
Notifications — Receive notifications for important ticket, onboarding, porting or system updates.
